Our Mission

A lifeline for women
ready to rise.

Women of Power walks beside women facing domestic violence, homelessness, and hardship — offering real help, real fast: safe housing, clothing, counseling connections, transportation, and a community that refuses to let them fall.

Compassion

Every woman is met with kindness, never judgment.
